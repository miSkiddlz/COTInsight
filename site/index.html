Data coverage & methodology

Historical views use currently published archives, which can contain later corrections. They are not archived release vintages or revision-free backtests.

Coverage: —. Dataset built: —.

Markets are identified by CFTC contract code, separately for each report type. Mini, Micro, exchange-specific and consolidated series are never merged. Turn off “Latest-report markets only” to include older series.

Net = Long − Short. Spreading positions count on both sides and do not add to Net. Net / OI is a signed net share of total open interest. “—” means missing data or a field not applicable to that category; it does not mean zero.

Dates refer to the position reference date, not the publication or download date. Changes compare consecutive available observations, with their dates shown above. Nonreportable positions are not exclusively retail traders. Green/red changes indicate direction, not profitability.

The chart and table use the source's All-maturities position fields. Other published columns, including changes, percentages, trader counts, concentration and Old/Other maturity fields where provided, remain in the complete source JSON. Each raw row references its original column schema; suppressed values are preserved.

Historical windows are calendar weeks and require at least 80% of expected weekly observations within a contiguous identical-unit segment. The percentile uses empirical midranks (equal observations share the middle rank). The COT range index is 100 × (current net − minimum net) / (maximum net − minimum net). It is undefined for a flat range. Both use net contract positions, include the selected observation, never future data, and describe this imported history only. Changing the chart scale does not change these metrics.
